Charleroi Airport (CRL)

Charleroi Brussels South is the closest airport for Hainaut (Ryanair, TUI, Wizz Air). We charge a single fare of €60 in a sedan (€80 in our minivan) from almost every town in our area. Mons, further away, is €75 (€95 by minivan).

At departure we drop you right in front of the terminal; the airport's paid drop-off zone is included. For most flights, plan to arrive 2 hours before take-off, and up to 3 hours during school holidays.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a transfer from Mons to Brussels Airport?
€120 in a sedan (up to 3 passengers) and €140 in our 7-seat minivan. The price is per vehicle, not per person, with tolls and parking included. To Charleroi Airport, the fare from Mons is €75.
How much is a transfer to Charleroi Airport?
€60 in a sedan and €80 in a minivan from almost every town in our area (La Louvière, Binche, Morlanwelz, Charleroi, Soignies, Thuin…). Mons is the only exception, at €75.
Do you pick up from SHAPE or Casteau?
Yes. Casteau and the SHAPE area are part of our regular zone: €60 to Charleroi Airport and €105 to Brussels Airport in a sedan. Send us the exact address and your flight time and we confirm the pickup time.
Do you drive at night and very early in the morning?
Yes, 24/7. A 4 a.m. pickup is normal for us, with no night, weekend or bank holiday surcharge.
What happens if my flight is delayed?
We track your flight in real time and adjust the pickup time at no extra cost. You don't need to call us.
Is this a taxi?
No, it is a private driver service booked in advance, with a fixed price agreed before the trip. There is no meter: the price you are given is the price you pay.
How many passengers and suitcases?
The sedan takes up to 3 passengers with their luggage. From 4 passengers, or with a lot of luggage, we use our minivan for up to 7 passengers. Child seats are available on request.
Can I book in English?
Yes. The easiest way is WhatsApp: write to us in English with your date, flight time, address, number of passengers and suitcases, and we reply with the price and pickup time.
How can I pay?
Cash, Bancontact, Payconiq or bank transfer. An invoice is available on request for business trips.
